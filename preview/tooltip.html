Design System / Tooltip

Tooltip

Short, contextual hints that appear on hover or keyboard focus. Two variants from Figma node 4388:31119: a simple label tooltip (4 placements with caret) and a rich tooltip with title, body, and CTA for deeper context. Background is #3A3A3A (soft dark grey) with white type.

Rich tooltip

Detailed context with a leading info icon, title, body, and Learn More action. 10 px radius, larger padding, optional CTA.

Do & Don't

Two rules cover 90% of tooltip misuse.
Do
  • Label every icon-only button.
  • Pair with aria-describedby on the trigger.
  • Show on focus, not just hover.
  • Use Rich tooltip only when a CTA earns the extra weight.
Don't
  • Put critical info in tooltips — touch users miss them.
  • Use tooltips for interactive content. Use a Popover.
  • Repeat the visible label.
  • Stack multiple tooltips at once.
