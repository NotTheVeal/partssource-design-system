ACCESSIBILITY & BEST PRACTICES

WCAG compliance and implementation guidance for inclusive border radius usage.
  • Consistency creates familiarity — use 4px radius for all standard buttons, inputs, and cards
  • Sharper corners (0-2px) for banners and alerts — edges align with viewport boundaries
  • Pill shapes (100px) for badges only — high radius on clickable elements can confuse users about hit area
  • Avoid excessive rounding — overly rounded corners can make small text harder to read near edges
  • Border radius doesn't affect accessibility directly, but consistency aids cognitive accessibility and learnability
