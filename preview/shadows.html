Design System / Shadows

Shadows

Three elevation levels for hierarchy and interactivity. Use shadow-sm for resting surfaces, shadow-md for hover-lifted cards, and shadow-lg for overlays. Shadows use Midnight (#002F48) at low opacity rather than pure black for warmth.

Shadow Scale

Three levels of elevation paired with corresponding offset and blur radius.

Accessibility & Best Practices

WCAG compliance and implementation guidance for inclusive shadow usage.
  • Shadows are purely visual — don't rely on elevation alone to convey hierarchy; pair with borders, spacing, or contrast
  • Elevation indicates interactivity — cards that lift on hover signal clickability; static content needs less shadow
  • Avoid excessive shadows — too many elevated surfaces creates visual noise and reduces clarity
  • Test on low-contrast displays — subtle shadows may disappear on certain screens; ensure borders provide fallback structure
  • Motion sensitivity: Animate shadow changes smoothly (200ms ease) to avoid jarring transitions
