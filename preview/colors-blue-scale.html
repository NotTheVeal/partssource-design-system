ACCESSIBILITY & BEST PRACTICES

WCAG compliance and implementation guidance for inclusive use of the blue scale.
  • PS Blue 500 (#005BA6) on white meets WCAG AA for body text (5.6:1) and AAA for large text — safe for primary CTAs and links
  • Don't use Cyan 200 (#009CF4) for body text on white (only 2.9:1) — reserve for large labels, icons against white, or text on dark blue
  • Light Blue 50 and Airway 100 are surface colors — never use them for text or icons; pair with PS Blue 500+ for foreground
  • Hover/Pressed pairs: Blue 700 (#004A84) and Blue 800 (#002F48) maintain ≥3:1 contrast against Blue 500 — meets WCAG 1.4.11 for state changes
  • Color blindness: Blue is the most universally distinguishable hue — safe for protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia users
  • Pair with non-color cues: Selected/active states using blue should also include underlines, weight changes, or icons — never color alone (WCAG 1.4.1)
