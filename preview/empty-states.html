Design System / Empty States

Empty States

When a view has nothing to show, an empty state explains why and tells the user what to do next. Three flavors cover almost every case: first-run (you haven't done X yet), no-results (your filter is too narrow), and error (something broke).

First-run

Greet the user; explain what the area will hold; give a clear primary action.

No quotes yet

When you request a quote from a vendor, it'll show up here. You can compare line items, add notes, and convert any quote into an order.

No results

Show what was searched, suggest broadening, and offer a one-click reset.

No parts match "DS-100A"

Try removing a filter, checking the spelling, or searching by manufacturer or item number.

Error

Tell the user what went wrong without exposing internals. Always offer a retry.

Couldn't load orders

Something went wrong on our end. Your data is safe — try refreshing in a few seconds.

Inline (table / card)

A more compact version for small surfaces.

No service events

This asset has no service history yet.

Permission needed

Ask your admin to grant view access to this catalog.

Voice

Empty states are the highest-leverage place to set the product's tone.
Do
  • Lead with the user's outcome, not the system's state.
  • One clear primary action + optional secondary.
  • Be specific about what's missing ("No parts match X").
  • Keep title under 6 words.
Don't
  • Use "Oops!" or other cute apology copy.
  • Expose error codes or stack traces to users.
  • Show three or more CTAs.
  • Use a sad face / illustration in clinical contexts.
