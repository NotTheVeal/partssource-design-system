ACCESSIBILITY & BEST PRACTICES

WCAG compliance and implementation guidance for inclusive semantic color usage.
  • Never use color alone — always pair semantic colors with icons (✓ ✗ ⚠ ⓘ) or descriptive text labels (WCAG 1.4.1)
  • Red/green confusion is the most common deficiency (8% of men) — Success and Danger must always include distinguishing icons
  • Danger #FF0000 on white meets 4.0:1 — passes AA for large text only; use darker red (#C8102E) for body-size error messages
  • Warning #E3A92D on white is 2.6:1 — use only on dark text (e.g., #4A4A4A on #FFF4D0 background) for warning copy
  • Success #17AB78 on white meets AA at 3.5:1 for large text; pair with darker green (#0F7A55) for body-size success messages
  • Light surface variants (#DCFAEF, #FACBCB, #FFF4D0) are background fills only — never use them as foreground/text colors
  • Status badges: Pair colored fills with text labels ("Success", "Error", "Warning") — color-only pills are inaccessible
  • Test with simulators: Verify status indicators remain distinguishable under protanopia, deuteranopia, and tritanopia simulations
