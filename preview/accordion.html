Accessibility

ARIA pattern + keyboard interaction.
  • Header is a real <button> with aria-expanded="true|false" and aria-controls pointing at the panel's id.
  • Panel is a sibling region; do not nest it inside the button.
  • Enter and Space both toggle. Tab moves focus into the panel when open.
  • Animations respect prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — duration drops to ~75 ms.
  • Don't hide form fields inside an accordion that's required to submit — users may not realize they're collapsed.

Do & Don't

Do
  • Use for scan-then-drill content (dashboards, FAQs).
  • Open the most relevant section by default.
  • Keep one section open at a time — opening another closes the current one.
  • Keep headers under 6 words.
Don't
  • Hide required form fields users must complete.
  • Use for primary navigation — use the left rail.
  • Nest accordions more than one level deep.
  • Animate longer than 300 ms.
