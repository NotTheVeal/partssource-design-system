Design System/Asset Detail Card

Asset Detail Card

1-for-1 with Figma node 4434:50731. Status Bar uses four states (Default · Operational · Limited · Hard Down). Modality icon is a 64 px rounded tile filled with one of those status colors and a white modality glyph pulled from /Modality-Iconography. Three card sizes: wide (4-column meta), mid (2-column meta), narrow (2-column meta below).

Modality Icon

Four state colors mapped to status: Default grey, Operational green, Limited orange, Hard Down red. Glyph pulled from /Modality-Iconography (CT shown).
