Design System / Sheets

Sheets

Right-side panels for edit, select, and detail flows where the user needs reference back to the page they came from. Sheets slide in from the right at 240 ms ease-out, use a lighter rgba(0,0,0,.32) scrim than modals, and persist the page-behind in a partially active state. Default desktop width is 550 px; tablet collapses to 450 px; mobile becomes a full-screen takeover.

Best practices

Layout & sizing

Right-side sheets on tablet (450 px landscape, 60% portrait); bottom sheets on phone. Never less than 320 px wide for a right-side panel — at that point switch to bottom. Don't scale the sheet content; reflow it.

Touch targets

Minimum 44 × 44 pt for any interactive element (Apple HIG) and 48 dp on Android. Pad the visual close icon to 44 px even though the glyph is 24. Stack form fields with at least 16 px gap on phones.

Gestures (mobile)

Bottom sheets dismiss with a swipe down. Drag-handle (grabber) above the title signals the gesture. Velocity > 0.5 px/ms or drag past 30% height = commit. Otherwise snap back. Tap-on-scrim also dismisses.

Action bar

Right-aligned row of buttons on desktop and tablet. On mobile, stack full-width with primary on top — thumb-zone reachable from a single grip. Sticky to the safe-area bottom so it never floats off when content scrolls.

Keyboard & safe area

When the soft keyboard appears, push the sheet up so the focused field stays visible — don't squish or overlap. Respect env(safe-area-inset-bottom) for the action bar on notched phones.

Content density

Reduce field labels to single line. Drop optional fields below a "More" disclosure. Replace 3-column metadata grids with 2-column on tablet, 1-column on mobile. Body padding shrinks 30 → 24 → 20 px as widths drop.

Scroll lock

Lock the underlying page scroll on phones (full-bleed bottom sheet) and on modal sheets. Don't lock on tablet detail/non-modal sheets — the user is still working with both surfaces. Use overscroll-behavior: contain.

Performance

Animate transform and opacity only. Lazy-load heavy content (search results, long lists) on open — defer until the slide-in completes (~280 ms). On low-end Android, drop scrim blur effects to keep 60 fps.

Common mistakes to avoid
  • Don't shrink-to-fit the desktop sheet on mobile. Reflow, don't scale.
  • Don't use a right-side sheet below 600 px — switch to bottom sheet.
  • Don't put primary CTA inline with cancel on phones. Stack and full-width.
  • Don't auto-dismiss a sheet after a timer. Sheets are user-driven.
  • Don't hide the action bar when the keyboard is up — float it above the keyboard.
  • Don't allow scroll chaining from the sheet to the page behind it.
  • Don't open a sheet inside a sheet. If you need a wizard, page through steps in one sheet.
  • Don't put critical errors below the fold. Pin them at the top of the body, above any inputs.

Accessibility

Sheets must be reachable by keyboard, dismissable, and announce themselves to assistive tech.

Roles & ARIA

Container is role="dialog" with aria-modal="true". The title gets aria-labelledby; the subtitle gets aria-describedby. Most select-style sheets are non-modal — use aria-modal="false" when the page-behind stays interactive.

Focus management

On open, focus the first form field (not the close button). On close, return focus to the trigger that opened the sheet. Trap Tab/Shift+Tab inside the sheet only when it's modal.

Keyboard

Esc always closes. Enter submits when focus is in a single-field form. Action buttons follow document tab order — primary CTA last (Cancel → Save).

Contrast

Title #005BA6 on #FFF = 7.4:1. Subtitle #4A4A4A = 8.6:1. Action bar #FAFAFA sits above body via the top border, not contrast alone.

Scroll containment

Sheet body is the only scrollable region. Don't lock the underlying page on non-modal sheets; do lock it on modal sheets. Mobile takeover always locks page scroll.

Hit targets

Close button is 24×24 visual but 40×40 effective touch. Drawer cards are 56 px tall minimum. Never put two interactive elements closer than 8 px on touch surfaces.

Do & Don’t

Sheet vs. modal vs. drawer — picking right prevents 80% of overlay problems.

Do

  • Use a sheet when the user needs to reference the page behind it while working.
  • Slide in from the right at 240 ms ease-out; slide out at the same speed.
  • Lead with a verb in the title — “Edit asset,” “Choose learners” — not “Asset details”.
  • Right-align actions; primary CTA on the right, secondary/cancel on the left.
  • Allow Esc, scrim-click, and the close icon to all dismiss the sheet.

Don’t

  • Use a sheet for a single yes/no decision — that’s a Dialog.
  • Stack sheets. If a sheet opens a sheet, split it into a wizard with steps instead.
  • Hide critical form errors below the scroll fold — pin them above the actions bar.
  • Use a sheet for a one-line confirmation — that’s a Toast.
  • Auto-dismiss a sheet after a timer. Sheets are user-driven.
